02The problem: stock you can't trust

Spreadsheets

No state machine, no audit trail. Two people edit, one number lies, nobody knows when it broke.

Heavy ERPs

Powerful but slow to adopt; small warehouses fall back to "the whiteboard and a guess".

The real cost

Phantom stock → failed deliveries → emergency purchases → silent margin loss.

What teams need: a ledger that decides the truth — not a balance someone edits.

StockSense02 / 10

03The solution: documents, not edits

4 document types

  • Receipts (vendor in)
  • Deliveries (customer out)
  • Internal transfers
  • Adjustments (counts)

Explicit workflow

  • Draft → Ready → Done
  • Waiting when stock is short
  • Manager sign-off to validate
  • Cancel releases reservations

Ledger-first truth

  • Every move is appended
  • Balances recomputed from moves
  • Full who/what/when trail
  • CSV export of the ledger

StockSense05 / 10

06Engineering depth

Capability RBAC

Every route declares a capability (validate_receipt, manage_users…) — staff book, managers sign off.

Reservation logic

Deliveries reserve stock when Ready; canceling releases it; short stock parks the doc in Waiting.

Self-healing balances

Balances recompute from the ledger; alerts sync after every stock-affecting action.

Verified

36 tests · clean tsc --noEmit · 40 routes build · typed confirmation for destructive tools
